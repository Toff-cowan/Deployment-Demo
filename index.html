GET READY TO GO LIVE

5 tips for your first deployment

Follow these steps before sharing your website with the world.

📁

1. Organize your files

Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js together in one project folder.

🧪

2. Test locally

Open your site in a browser. Check buttons, links, images, and mobile layouts.

🔐

3. Protect secrets

Never place passwords, API secrets, or private keys in frontend files.

☁️

4. Choose a host

Try GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel to publish this static website.

🔗

5. Share and improve

Visit your live URL, test it again, and share it with a classmate for feedback.
